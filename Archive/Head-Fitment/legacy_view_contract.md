# Legacy View Contract

The generative implementation accepted the eight established body/head views: Front, Front-Left-3-4, Front-Right-3-4, Left-Profile, Right-Profile, Back-Left-3-4, Back-Right-3-4, and Back.

For every view, the Reference Body controlled only the fitted neck width, axis, and cut position. The Character Head controlled head pose, face, hair, expression, and identity. Three-quarter views additionally locked the head and neck as one orientation and prohibited viewer-facing eye drift, mirroring, twisting, or drift into front, back, or profile views.
