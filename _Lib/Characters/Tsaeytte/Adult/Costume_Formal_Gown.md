# Costume Description

## Costume Description - Just the Facts

<!-- ZET:BEGIN COSTUME_DESCRIPTION_FACTS -->

* Costume name: `Deep Woodland Formal Dress`.
* Overall silhouette: elegant sleeveless forest-formal halter maxi dress with high turtleneck collar, waist cutout, fitted upper bodice, decorated waist band, ruched hip panel, and full floor-length skirt.
* Top/upper garment: deep woodland green sleeveless halter bodice; high turtleneck collar; shoulders and arms bare; fitted matte cloth; subtle vertical structure; delicate gold-olive vine embroidery on the front bodice.
* Midsection/waist: narrow horizontal waist cutout above the skirt; decorative gold-olive elvish waist band integrated into the garment; small centered leaf-knot ornament at the front waist; no separate belt.
* Lower garment: full flowing maxi skirt in deep woodland green; same length all around; the hem hangs consistently approximately one inch above the floor around the entire circumference; it never becomes shorter in front, never forms a train, and never drags on the floor; the skirt hem partially obscures the boots while the bottoms of the boots remain visible beneath the hem.
* Legwear: none visible.
* Footwear: brown leather heeled adventuring boots worn under the dress; the hem partially obscures the boots, but the bottoms of the boots remain visible beneath the hem; preserve sturdy boot shape when visible.
* Jewelry: two to four loose narrow gold bangles on each wrist; bangles gather naturally near the wrist when arms hang down.
* Equipment: none.
* Materials: soft matte formal cloth; subtle woven texture; brown leather boots; warm gold metal bangles; gold-olive embroidery.
* Colors: dominant deep woodland green; darker forest-green shadow folds; muted gold-olive embroidery and waist decoration; warm brown leather footwear; warm gold jewelry.
* Fit: fitted halter bodice and collar; fitted waist; ruched hip panel; loose flowing formal skirt.
* Forbidden drift: no gray dress, no barefoot feet, no exposed full boots, no ankle-length skirt, no high-low hem, no train, no trailing fabric, no cape, no armor, no weapons, no pouches, no satchel, no large props, no asymmetrical costume features, no extra sleeves, no gloves.
