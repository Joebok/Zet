# Costume Description

## Costume Description - Just the Facts

<!-- ZET:BEGIN IDENTITY_PRESERVATION_COSTUME -->

* Costume name: `Canonical Adventure Gear`.
* Overall silhouette: elegant mobile sorceress-adventurer outfit with teal fabric, exposed shoulders, visible midriff, high-low skirt, dark leggings, tall high-heeled boots, and a utility belt.
* Top/upper garment: teal off-shoulder ruffled crop top with fitted bodice and decorative seams; rich dark teal fabric with subtle gold or star-like ornamentation.
* Sleeves: short separate off-shoulder puff sleeves gathered around the upper arms only; each sleeve ends clearly above the elbow.
* Sleeve length rule: elbows, forearms, wrists, and hands remain completely uncovered. Do not extend the sleeves to the elbow, forearm, wrist, or hand.
* Neckline: off-shoulder neckline below the shoulders; ruffled upper edge; no straps.
* Midsection/waist: exposed midriff and navel between crop top and belt/skirt; broad brown leather utility belt at the waist; adult non-explicit fantasy styling.
* Lower garment: teal high-low skirt with three fixed length zones: shortest open front, longer side panels, and longest back panels.
* High-low skirt construction: the front is a high arched opening that exposes the dark leggings from upper thigh through knees, lower legs, and boots; the side panels fall longer; the back panel is the longest drape.
* Legwear: dark navy leggings visible beneath the skirt opening; plain or subtly paneled, not decorative enough to compete with the skirt.
* Footwear: tall warm brown leather high-heeled adventuring boots with straps, panels, buckles, and worn travel texture; the high heels are canonical and must be preserved.
* Jewelry: layered dark necklace with central blue-violet teardrop pendant; matching dangling blue-violet earrings with gold settings.
* Materials: teal cloth, gold embroidery or trim, brown leather, dark leggings, metal buckles, polished blue-violet stones.
* Colors: dominant teal and dark teal, dark navy leggings, warm brown leather, gold accents, violet-blue jewelry.
* Fit: tailored, mobile, theatrical adventuring wear; elegant but practical enough for travel; clothing follows the character's existing anatomy rather than changing the body.
* Forbidden drift: no armor plates, corset-only redesign, long formal gown, modern clothing, visible straps, cape, sword, staff, wand, bow, shield, backpack, spellbook, glowing artifact, extra weapons, long sleeves, wrist cuffs, gloves, hidden leggings, hidden boots, symmetrical long skirt, or different color palette unless explicitly requested.
