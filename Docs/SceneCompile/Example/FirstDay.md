## Canonical Art Style

<!-- ZET:BEGIN CANONICAL_ART_STYLE -->

Canonical Art Style: Painterly semi-realistic, anime-influenced facial proportions, etc.

Dialog panel rules

Dialogue appears inside compact rectangular parchment dialogue panels with softly rounded corners. The panels are only slightly larger than the text they contain, leaving small, even margins on all sides. They are never oversized or excessively padded.

Panel backgrounds are warm ivory parchment rather than pure white, with a subtle paper texture and a thin dark bronze border.

Each dialogue panel has a short, unobtrusive triangular pointer aimed toward the speaking character's mouth.

Dialogue panels are integrated naturally into the composition and never obscure important characters, faces, or important scene details.

Size each dialogue panel to fit its dialogue closely. Do not create large floating caption boxes or excessive empty space inside the panel.

Lettering uses a clean, modern comic-style sans-serif. Medium weight. Crisp edges. High legibility. Sentence case. Normal capitalization. Appropriate for a graphic novel rather than a children's picture book.

Wrap dialogue naturally into two or three balanced lines when practical. Avoid overly long single lines and avoid awkward one-word final lines.

Dialogue panels should complement the artwork and feel like part of the illustrated page rather than an afterthought.

<!-- ZET:END CANONICAL_ART_STYLE -->
