# Draft and test a costume

1. Select the universe, character, and phase, then open **Costumes > Costume Wizard**. Give the costume a name and enter a short **Costume summary**, such as “Fitted indigo travel coat over charcoal trousers, with brown ankle boots and a brass clasp.” Mention deliberate changes from the references.
2. Leave **Include equipment, jewelry, and props** checked when those items are part of the costume. Clear it to skip that interview and omit removable items such as earrings, a satchel, or a hand-held prop. Integral closures and trim still belong in the costume.
3. Add one to three references with **Choose image**, **Paste image**, or **Image Inventory**, then caption each with what it contributes, such as “coat cut and layering” or “boot and buckle detail.” Click **Create Costume Draft**.

## Review the interviews

| Section | Check the bullets for |
| --- | --- |
| Costume at a glance | One opening sentence with the silhouette, main layers, and dominant colors, then the signature design details. |
| Upper body and waist | Neckline, sleeves, layering, fit, waistline, closures, fabrics, and trim supported by the references or your direction. |
| Lower body and footwear | Shape, length, drape, legwear, and shoes or boots. Use a separate `Footwear:` bullet for the footwear and `Footwear Contact:` only for unusual ground contact. |
| Equipment, jewelry, and props | One item per bullet, with size, attachment and anatomical side where known. Start with a label such as `Jewelry:`, `Equipment:`, or `Containers:`. |

Keep each bullet short. Use “brooch on the wearer’s left lapel” for stable placement. Save profile visibility, overlap, and hidden construction for review of the relevant Dressing view.

Use **Save Draft** after manual edits. For a proposed revision, enter direction on the relevant section and click **Refine section**, then check **Accept suggested replacement** and save if it is right. If a section needs attention, retry it or supply the facts yourself. Use **Leave section empty** for an optional section with no usable evidence. To return later, choose the entry under **Resume a draft** and click **Resume**.

## Test FRONT and accept

1. Open **Preview Costume.md** and check the saved text. The view-specific sections start empty. When accessories are skipped, check that the general and identity sections describe garments only.
2. Lock and publish **Character Assembly FRONT** for the same character and phase before clicking **Test Render**. Both Draft and Final Assembly locks are valid. Wait for the private FRONT image to load.
3. Compare the private result with the costume references for silhouette, layering, materials, footwear, and enabled accessories. Compare it with Assembly for identity, body proportions, pose, and framing. If a general fact needs correction, revise its interview section, check the preview, and test the new revision.
4. Click **Accept Costume** to save the template. The confirmation points to **Assets > Costume-Dressing** for production candidates. Test renders are labeled with their draft revision and open in the shared zoom viewer. Use **Post Render Adjustments** on a rendered view for view-specific changes.

## Zet Tester checkpoints

Use the isolated Zet Test universe selected by the case. Map `costume_extra_info`, when supplied, to **Costume summary**. Verify the reference preview and fill its caption before submitting. Use the checked default unless the case requests accessory omission. Record the effective Costume Wizard model, toggle setting, section status and any exact failure, the saved preview, each completed private render and revision, and costume acceptance. Report visible successes and mismatches separately from workflow completion. Inspect the existing task before repeating a submission after a timeout.
