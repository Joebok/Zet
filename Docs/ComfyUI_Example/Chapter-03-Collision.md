## Scene Description

The boys have just barreled into Tsaeytte, knocking her to the ground. Her books have scattered across the stone pathway.

Tsaeytte sits on the ground on the right side of the image, close to the foreground. She has fallen backward with her legs bent in front of her and both hands behind her supporting her weight. Several books are scattered around her, some lying open face-down, others spread across the path. She stares at the books in shock, trying not to cry. Her eyes are wet and her expression is on the verge of tears.

The three boys occupy the left foreground. They have already passed Tsaeytte and are walking toward and past the viewer, exiting the scene. They are very close to the camera, large in the frame, with their bodies angled toward the viewer as they continue down the path. Their movement clearly carries them out of the foreground, leaving Tsaeytte behind them.

They remain completely caught up in their horseplay, still laughing, shoving, and jostling one another as if nothing happened.

Kaeldor walks on the far left of the group. Without slowing or turning his body, he turns his head only slightly toward Tsaeytte. His eyes remain directed forward toward the path ahead rather than meeting hers. Almost as an afterthought he mutters, "sorry..."

Valindia is visible behind the departing boys, farther back on the left side of the image near the archway. She has stopped walking and turned back toward the accident. Her expression is one of genuine surprise and concern. Her mouth forms a small "O" and her eyes are wide. Both hands have instinctively begun to rise toward her face, fingers spread and palms inward. Although she had been dismissive only moments earlier, her reaction makes it clear she did not expect this to happen and is genuinely alarmed.
