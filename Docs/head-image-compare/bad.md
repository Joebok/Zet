# Render Task: HEAD-IMAGE Elder CHARACTER REFERENCE

Create one image of Tsaeytte in the Elder phase, shown in direct front view.

Use the attached source image as the identity reference for the same person at an earlier Adult phase.

Render the same recognizable character as an older Elder-phase version of herself.

The primary goal of this task is successful age transformation while preserving identity.

## Source relationship

Use the source image to preserve Tsaeytte's recognizable identity:
- eye shape and spacing;
- vivid violet-purple eyes;
- basic nose shape;
- mouth shape;
- heart-shaped facial identity;
- small pointed chin;
- pointed elf ears;
- characteristic asymmetry.

Do not preserve the source image's youthful age presentation.

Do not preserve the source black chin-length hairstyle.

The source image is an identity reference, not an age lock and not a hairstyle lock.

## Requested view

Use anatomical left and right.

Render the head in direct front view, facing the viewer squarely. The requested target view overrides any source-image orientation.

- Show both eyes clearly.
- Preserve the front-view facial visibility appropriate to a direct front view.

## Elder transformation

This must be a visibly older version of the same person.

The final face must read as Elder Tsaeytte even if the silver hair is ignored.

Silver hair alone is not enough.

Apply clear but restrained elven aging.

Required Elder changes:

- make the face slightly longer, finer-boned, and more mature;
- make the cheekbones read more clearly;
- reduce youthful cheek fullness;
- add modest hollowing beneath the cheekbones;
- add restrained under-eye maturity;
- make the lower face slightly narrower and more refined;
- reduce youthful softness around the mouth and jaw;
- add faint but visible lines around the eyes;
- add faint but visible lines around the mouth;
- add subtle mature skin texture and tonal variation;
- make the complexion paler and cooler than the Adult source.

She must still look graceful, luminous, and attractive, but clearly older.

Do not leave the face young-adult.

Do not render her as deeply wrinkled, sagging, sickly, skeletal, frail, or humanly ancient.

## Elder hair

Replace the source hair with Elder Tsaeytte's canonical hair:

- luminous silver;
- shoulder-length asymmetrical long bob;
- left-side part;
- heavier sweep across the right side of the forehead;
- elongated face-framing locks;
- soft, lightly tousled, gently layered texture;
- graceful inward-curving ends.

The hair should clearly read as longer than the Adult chin-length bob.

## Rendering Style

Use the Canonical Art Style:

Painterly semi-realistic fantasy illustration with anime-influenced facial proportions, and large expressive eyes.

## Negative guidance

Do not let the style erase the Elder age cues.

Avoid:
- unchanged young-adult face with only silver hair;
- generic anime face replacement;
- black hair;
- chin-length adult bob;
- human-style extreme old age;
- deep wrinkles, sagging jowls, or frailty;
- sickly or corpse-like aging;
- wrong eye color;
- wrong ear shape;
- wrong view;
- narrative scene or extra characters.
