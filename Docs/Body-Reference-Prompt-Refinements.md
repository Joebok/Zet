Compiler Nuance Requested

The compiler should distinguish between these task types:

body-reference

Use technical fitment shell. Do not include costume, weapon, pouch, jewelry, or equipment placement rules except as negative constraints against adding them.

head-fitment

Preserve head identity, face, eyes, hair, ears, and neck alignment. Body shell/bust instructions may appear, but full-body proportion instructions should be minimal unless needed.

costume-fitment

Use established body-reference proportions. Include costume rules. Emphasize that the costume adapts to Tsaeytte’s body, not the other way around.

turnaround

Include costume and equipment placement rules. Keep pose and proportions consistent between views.

expression sheet

Prioritize face identity, expression, eyes, ears, hair, and age. Avoid body/costume over-specification unless the expression sheet includes full-body poses.

narrative scene

Allow scene, action, mood, props, and lighting only when explicitly requested. Identity anchors should remain protected from being obscured by scene complexity.
