# Head-Image Edit operations

Operation wording for the independent pipeline. Character-specific visibility and anatomy belong in HEAD_EDIT_* character sections.

<!-- ZET:BEGIN HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_FRONT -->
Create one upright, eye-level frontal head portrait, facing the viewer.
<!-- ZET:END HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_FRONT -->

<!-- ZET:BEGIN HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_FRONT_LEFT_3_4 -->
Edit Image 1 to show the same head from its anatomical left side in front three-quarter view, approximately 45 degrees from front. The nose points screen-left; the anatomical-left cheek and ear are nearest the camera on screen-right.
<!-- ZET:END HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_FRONT_LEFT_3_4 -->

<!-- ZET:BEGIN HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_FRONT_RIGHT_3_4 -->
Edit Image 1 to show the same head from its anatomical right side in front three-quarter view, approximately 45 degrees from front. The nose points screen-right; the anatomical-right cheek and ear are nearest the camera on screen-left.
<!-- ZET:END HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_FRONT_RIGHT_3_4 -->

<!-- ZET:BEGIN HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_LEFT_PROFILE -->
Edit Image 1 by rotating the whole head 90 degrees toward screen-left into a true anatomical-left profile. The nose points screen-left. The far side is occluded by the head and hair.
<!-- ZET:END HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_LEFT_PROFILE -->

<!-- ZET:BEGIN HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_RIGHT_PROFILE -->
Edit Image 1 by rotating the whole head 90 degrees toward screen-right into a true anatomical-right profile. The nose points screen-right. The far side is occluded by the head and hair.
<!-- ZET:END HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_RIGHT_PROFILE -->

<!-- ZET:BEGIN HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_BACK_LEFT_3_4 -->
Edit Image 1 to show the same head from behind and on its anatomical left side, a rear-left three-quarter view approximately 135 degrees from front. The near anatomical-left ear is on screen-left. Reproject the entire head, hair and attached ears together.
<!-- ZET:END HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_BACK_LEFT_3_4 -->

<!-- ZET:BEGIN HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_BACK_RIGHT_3_4 -->
Edit Image 1 to show the same head from behind and on its anatomical right side, a rear-right three-quarter view approximately 135 degrees from front. The near anatomical-right ear is on screen-right. Reproject the entire head, hair and attached ears together.
<!-- ZET:END HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_BACK_RIGHT_3_4 -->

<!-- ZET:BEGIN HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_BACK -->
Edit Image 1 to show the same head directly from behind, rotated 180 degrees from front. Keep the back of the skull and nape square to the camera, with the face pointing straight away.
<!-- ZET:END HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_BACK -->

<!-- ZET:BEGIN HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_FRONT_SOURCE -->
Edit Image 1 into an upright eye-level FRONT head reference of the same phase. Preserve the character's established appearance.
<!-- ZET:END HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_FRONT_SOURCE -->

<!-- ZET:BEGIN HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_FRONT_PHASE_CHANGE -->
Edit Image 1 into an upright eye-level FRONT head reference of the target phase. Apply the authored phase changes while retaining the same person's identity.
<!-- ZET:END HEAD_EDIT_OPERATION_FRONT_PHASE_CHANGE -->
