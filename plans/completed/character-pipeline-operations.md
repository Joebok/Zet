# Responsive character-pipeline operations
Status: complete
Related specification: user-approved plan in the originating conversation.

## Goal and constraints
Provide durable, promptly acknowledged preparation for generation and review actions across the five major-character pipelines. Keep the UI usable during preparation and expose status after navigation. Preserve universe isolation, locked-image integrity, frozen ordered references, attempt fencing, existing handoff and recovery, and priority Stop. Do not add permanent tests, dependencies, live calls, deployment, or unrelated workflows.

## Current behavior
`CharacterRenderSlots.run_available` loads details and prepares candidates under the batch-state lock before the HTTP response. `local_asset_pipeline.js` reports success only after that response. Existing slot workers and AI workflows handle downstream execution and recovery.
