- [front_torso_visible] Front midsection: a broad section of bare abdomen remains visible, including the navel; the blouse ends well above the belt.
- Belt: wide dark-brown leather belt worn low on the hips rather than at the natural waist.
- [frontish] Belt front: a large rectangular antique-brass buckle is centered at the front.
- Lower garment: deep-teal high-low overskirt attached at the low hip belt and worn over fitted dark navy leggings.

  - [frontish,profiles] the hem length increases continuously from the open front edges toward the center back;
